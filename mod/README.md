# 初始岛生存 2.0

《饥荒》单机海难独岛生存模组。地图正常生成，只能在出生岛和海上活动；赌博机岛可以单独开放。火山及其他世界入口禁用。豹卷风出现概率100%。

**请从新建海难世界开始使用2.0，不沿用旧版本存档。启用或更新后重启游戏。**

非船长开局获得幸运帽；除船长和奶奶外额外获得铲子。思维帽可以制作幸运帽与海腿号。

金块保底：非奶奶角色，本岛前4次宝藏都没出过金块，第5次使用含3金块的原版预设。铲子、风向标、船炮和爆炸等方式共用记录；此前出过金块，即使用完也不触发。只保底一次，关闭开关也记录挖宝结果。

宝石倍率只影响幸运帽宝藏的出现概率，最高100%。70天季节为20/15/20/15天。蜘蛛巢只搬动不足的数量，不生成新巢。退格键显示尚未结束的海妖、季节事件和智慧果倒计时，雨季首雨为估计值，面板使用英文。

哈姆雷特扩展需要安装对应DLC，仍只在海难生效：铸铁块拆为4铁矿；挖宝怪物原6种与新增14种等概率抽取，怪物总量和事件概率不变，最小利齿幼苗及不带电雷鸟；本岛至少3栋合格建筑后沿用原版盗贼规则，藏宝限本岛；新建玩家市政厅自带市长；部分资源宝藏加入1至2个修剪物及荧光果；奇物店可出售50呼噜币的守护者之角、30呼噜币的河鹿角。

## English

Single-player Shipwrecked only. Start a new world for version 2.0; earlier mod saves are not supported. Restart after enabling or updating.

Defaults: Auto language; no daily Octopus King cooldown for non-food items without freshness; gold guarantee on; casino landing off; 2x Lucky Hat yellow/green gem chance; a 70-day year (20/15/20/15); at least 1 coastal den; Fishbone suction on. Choose 0 to 5 coastal dens. All six optional Hamlet additions are off by default and require installed Hamlet content.

Non-Woodlegs characters receive a Lucky Hat; characters other than Woodlegs and Wickerbottom also receive a Shovel. The Brain of Thought unlocks Lucky Hat and Sea Legs recipes.

Except Wickerbottom, if the first four home-island excavations yield no gold, the fifth uses a vanilla preset with 3 nuggets. All excavation methods share saved world history. Earlier treasure gold prevents the guarantee even if spent. The one-time history is recorded while the option is off. Gem chances affect Lucky Hat treasures only and cap at 100%. Coastal dens are moved only to meet the shortfall; no new dens are created. Backspace displays active timers in English; first Monsoon rain is estimated.

Optional Hamlet features: Alloy deconstruction into 4 Iron Ore; 14 small attacking creatures sharing the original treasure monster slots equally with the original 6; native town bandits after 3 qualifying home-island buildings, with treasure restricted to home; a mayor in new player Town Halls; Clippings/Light Bulbs in some resource treasures (1–2 each when selected); Guardian's Horn/Hippopotamoose Antler at the Antiques shop for 50/30 Oincs.
